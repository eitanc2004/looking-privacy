Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026

Looking is an app for sharing outfit posts and personal style with the people closest to you. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you control it. We've written it in plain language on purpose.

01

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Looking, we collect:

  • Account details — your username, display name, email address, and a hashed version of your password (we never store passwords in plain text)
  • Photos — images you choose to upload when creating a post
  • Captions and comments — text you write on posts and in comments
  • Social connections — who you follow and who follows you
  • In-app activity — reactions, group memberships, and notification interactions

We do not collect your device location, contacts, or any data beyond what you explicitly provide inside the app.

02

Device Permissions

Looking requests the following permissions on your device. You will be prompted before any permission is granted, and you can change your choices at any time in your device Settings.

  • Camera — used only when you tap the camera button to take a photo for a new post. We do not access your camera at any other time.
  • Photo Library — used only when you choose to upload an existing photo from your library for a new post. We do not scan or read your library in the background.

Photos you choose to upload are sent to our servers and stored via Supabase (see section 04). Photos that remain on your device are never accessed without your explicit action.

03

How We Use Your Information

Everything we collect is used solely to run and improve Looking:

  • To show your posts to your followers and to you
  • To power the Explore feed for public posts
  • To send notifications when someone follows you, reacts to your post, or leaves a comment
  • To enforce your privacy settings (private account, group-only posts)
  • To let you manage your account and content

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not build profiles for third-party use.

04

Third Parties We Work With

We use a small number of trusted services to run Looking:

  • Supabase — stores the photos you upload, hosted securely in the cloud
  • Expo / EAS — delivers app updates to your device
  • Render — hosts the Looking API server

These services receive only the minimum data needed to perform their function. They are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

05

How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from within the app, we permanently remove your profile, posts, comments, reactions, and all associated data from our servers. This cannot be undone.

Backups may retain data for a short period after deletion as part of routine infrastructure operations. This data is never accessible to other users and is purged on the next backup cycle.

06

Your Rights and Controls

You are in control of your data:

  • Private account — switch to private in Settings to require approval before anyone can follow you or see your posts
  • Delete posts — remove any post at any time from the post detail screen
  • Delete your account — permanently erase your account and all your data from Settings › Edit Profile › Delete Account
  • Access your data — email us and we will provide a copy of the data associated with your account
07

Children's Privacy

Looking is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

08

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also notify you within the app. Continuing to use Looking after a change means you accept the updated policy.
